# Code of Conduct

Aureform is a place to build useful interfaces together. People with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience should be able to participate safely and respectfully.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code with care. Give concrete feedback and make room for questions.
- Respect other people's time, identities, experience and boundaries.
- Credit contributions and correct mistakes openly.
- Keep conversations relevant to the work. English and Spanish are both welcome.
- Ask before sharing another person's private information or private messages.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, impersonation and repeated disruption are not acceptable. Neither is retaliating against someone for raising a concern.

Disagreement about an implementation is normal. Personal attacks and continued contact after someone has asked you to stop are not.

## Scope

These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, reviews and other community spaces managed by the project, including interactions where someone is representing Aureform.

## Responding to concerns

Maintainers may remove harmful content, ask participants to change their behavior, close a discussion or restrict participation. They should explain their action when doing so is safe and should consider the impact and context of the behavior.

For content on GitHub, use GitHub's **Report content** option. For an immediate conversation problem, ask a maintainer to intervene without repeating private information or abusive material. Do not use public issues to publish identifying details about affected people.

These reporting options are not an emergency service. The project does not promise a response time or a separate confidential conduct-reporting inbox.
